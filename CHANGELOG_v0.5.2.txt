Whisper Files v0.5.2
- fixed the real cause of subtitles starting at 00:00 when speech begins later
- enabled whisper.cpp built-in Silero VAD v6.2.0 for every transcription chunk
- bundled the ~0.88 MB VAD model inside the APK; no separate VAD download is required
- Whisper segment/token timestamps remain mapped to the original input timeline after VAD trimming
- first detected VAD speech start is used as a hard lower bound for the first segment/word timestamps
- preserved positive MP4 audio-track presentation timestamp offset, preventing systematic early subtitles in files whose audio track starts after video time 0
- kept token timestamps for karaoke/word highlighting
- VAD parameters tuned for subtitles: threshold 0.50, min speech 120 ms, min silence 180 ms, speech pad 30 ms
