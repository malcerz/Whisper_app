Whisper Files v0.3
- subtitle editor with previous/next navigation and live final-layout preview
- edited text is saved back to the word timeline and SRT/TXT
- subtitles are split by measured pixel width instead of a fixed character count
- 84% safe horizontal area prevents text from leaving portrait video edges
- portrait videos use shorter cues (up to ~2.5 s / 6 words) and max 2 lines
- whisper.cpp token timestamps are enabled and stored as word-level timing
- active spoken word can be highlighted and enlarged during burned-in subtitles
- active-word enlargement adjustable from 100% to 140%
- active-word highlight strength adjustable from 0% to 100%
- active-word tracking can be disabled
- editor and Media3 burn use the same SubtitleLayoutEngine and SubtitlePainter
- legacy v0.2 SRT can be converted to an approximate word timeline without retranscription
