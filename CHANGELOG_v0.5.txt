Whisper Files v0.5
- subtitle size is now a percentage slider (2.0-10.0% of the video height) instead of Małe/Średnie/Duże
- subtitle position is now a percentage slider (0% = top, 100% = bottom) instead of Dół/Środek/Góra
- new highlight colour picker for the currently spoken word (yellow, orange, red, green, cyan, pink)
- highlight strength slider still blends the chosen colour with white
- old Małe/Średnie/Duże and Dół/Środek/Góra settings are migrated to the equivalent percentages
- the subtitle editor, the live preview and the Media3 burn keep using the same SubtitlePainter and SubtitleLayoutEngine
- subtitle block is clamped so it never leaves the video frame at any size/position combination
